# Brand Guidelines — Reclame Bureau Eva

## Voice & Tone
- Always write in an active, direct voice. Avoid passive constructions.
- Tone adapts per channel: warm and personal on social, precise and confident in email, bold and punchy in OOH/taglines.
- Never use jargon unless the audience uses it themselves. Earn credibility through specificity, not buzzwords.
- Sustainability claims must be verifiable. Never use "eco-friendly", "green", or "sustainable" without a concrete proof point attached.
- Humour is allowed but never at the expense of the audience. Self-aware beats sarcastic.

## Brand Personality
- Intelligent without being elitist
- Warm without being soft
- Ambitious without being aggressive
- Honest to the point of being disarming

## Writing Rules
1. Lead with the benefit, not the feature.
2. One idea per sentence. One message per asset.
3. Subject lines: under 60 characters, curiosity-gap or clear value.
4. CTAs: specific action verb + outcome. "Discover your first origin" beats "Learn more".
5. Headlines: if it could belong to any brand, rewrite it.
6. Avoid: "game-changing", "revolutionary", "seamless", "holistic", "leverage", "synergy".

## Visual Identity (copy guidelines)
- Headlines: sentence case only. No ALL CAPS in body copy.
- Numbers under 10 are written out (one, two, three). 10 and above use numerals.
- Em dashes (—) for parenthetical emphasis. No double hyphens.
- Oxford comma always.

## Audience Principles
- Respect the audience's intelligence. They can tell when they're being sold to.
- Speak to aspirations, not insecurities — unless the product genuinely solves a pain point.
- Validate before persuading. Acknowledge what they already know or feel before introducing the brand's perspective.

## Channel Guidelines

### Email
- Subject line + preview text must work together as a unit.
- First sentence carries the most weight — no preamble, no "Hi [Name], we hope you're well."
- Body: 150–200 words max for acquisition emails. Longer for nurture sequences.
- One CTA per email. Place it after the key argument, not before.

### Social (Instagram / LinkedIn)
- Instagram: lead with a visual hook in the first line. Use line breaks generously.
- LinkedIn: open with a tension or insight, not a product pitch.
- Hashtags: max 8 on Instagram. Zero on LinkedIn unless trending/event-specific.

### Taglines
- Must work without context. Tested by: can a stranger understand who this is for?
- Aim for under 7 words.
- Avoid rhyme unless it feels completely natural.
- Should survive translation into Dutch, French, and German.
